Three identical bores.

An equal tee has the same bore on the run and the branch, so flow splits or merges symmetrically. We make them to ASME B16.9 in A234 WPB and to EN 10253-1 in S235.

ASME B16.9

A234 WPB, seamless, wall STD or XS, painted.

EN 10253-1

S235, seamless or welded, black or galvanized.
