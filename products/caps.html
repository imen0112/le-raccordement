A clean, tested termination.

Our dished-end caps are cold-formed from the same seamless pipe used in the rest of the line, so wall thickness and grade match perfectly at the weld seam. Each batch is dimensionally inspected, marked by micro-percussion, and can be hydrostatic-tested before dispatch.

Pressure-tested

Hydrostatic test reports issued on request for critical service.

Mill traceability

Material certificate 3.1 to EN 10204, heat number traceable to raw pipe.
