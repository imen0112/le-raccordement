Geometries & technical background

Pick the right branch.

An equal tee has three identical bores, flow splits or merges symmetrically. A reducing tee uses a smaller branch outlet when the off-take line carries less volume. We produce both to ASME B16.9 dimensional standards.

Equal tee

Same bore on run and branch, symmetric flow distribution.

Reducing tee

Smaller branch for asymmetric off-take lines and process headers.

Special outlets, lateral tees, oversized branches.

Tell us the run and branch, the material, the standard. We'll come back with availability and price.
